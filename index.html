Facebook payslip to CSV

Drop Facebook UK payslip PDFs here. Each one becomes a set of transaction rows for a payroll account: gross pay as income, tax, National Insurance and pension as outflows, and the net pay as an outflow you match to the real bank deposit in your finance app. Everything is parsed in your browser; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Export CSV

Copy the CSV, save it as a .csv file and import it into your finance app against a dedicated payroll account (a manual checking account in the payslip currency, opening balance 0). Columns are date,amount,name,currency,category,tags,account,notes; inflows are positive and outflows negative.

If no file appeared, this view blocks downloads. Open the page from its own file instead, or use Copy CSV and save the text as a .csv file.

Each payslip nets to zero in the payroll account: gross pay in, deductions out, and a final "Net pay" outflow equal to the deposit that reaches your bank. Match that outflow with the bank deposit as a transfer in your finance app, so the salary is counted once, as gross income, with the deductions shown as expenses.
